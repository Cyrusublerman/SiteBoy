# Sonification of Climate Change
**Status:** BRAINSTORM | **Cluster:** audio-waves


Map climate datasets to audio sources to produce a data-driven generative composition.

## Concepts

- **Rhythm** encodes time dimension (e.g. year → beat subdivision, seasonal cycle → bar length).
- **Sound source selection** by phenomenological fit — sounds that intuitively evoke the data domain (e.g. crackling for fire frequency, water for precipitation, low drone for sea-level rise).
- **Parameter mapping** — define the numerical range of each variable and map to audio parameters (pitch, amplitude, tempo, timbre, filter cutoff).

## Questions to resolve

- What datasets are in scope (temperature anomaly, CO₂ ppm, sea level, ice extent, fire frequency, extreme weather events)?
- What is the output medium — live performance, rendered audio, interactive web tool?
- How are multiple concurrent variables layered without masking?
- Is there a reference year to use as a sonic baseline?
