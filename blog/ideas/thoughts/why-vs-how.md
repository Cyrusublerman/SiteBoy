# Why Is How's Baggage
**Status:** BRAINSTORM


A philosophical fragment on the distinction between *why* and *how*.

---

It is difficult to answer *why* without collapsing into *how*.

When asked why something is, most responses describe chronology or mechanism — the sequence of events that produced the outcome, or the physical/logical process that sustains it.

In science, *why* typically means: describe the mechanism to a given level of abstraction. At each level, the answer is another *how*.

**Proposed distinction:** *Why* requires relevance to the questioner's context. It is not a request for mechanism — it is a request for the cause-and-effect chain that is actionable or meaningful for a specific goal. *How* is mechanism-agnostic; *why* is goal-indexed.

**Consequence:** Without an ulterior motive or greater goal on the part of the asker, *why* has no determinate answer. *Why* is not a feature of the world — it is a feature of the relationship between the world and a situated agent's intentions.

**Implication:** Answering *why* correctly requires knowing what the asker is trying to do or exploit. A *why* without a goal reduces to a *how*.
