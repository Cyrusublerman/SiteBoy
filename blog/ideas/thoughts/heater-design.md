# Heater Design
**Status:** BRAINSTORM


Resistance heater with a sculptural form factor.

## Concepts

- **Spiral column:** Thin coiled resistance wire wound in a tight helix to form a near-vertical pole — analogous to outdoor patio heaters but condensed to a single column.
- **Ceramic shell:** Perforated ceramic casing over the pole. Possible forms: honeycomb lattice, gumnut texture, open stocking mesh.
- **Straight pole variant:** Simpler form with no spiral — just a vertical heated rod encased in perforated ceramic.

## Speculative extensions

- Magnetic levitation of the heating element with wireless (inductive) power transfer — eliminates physical contact, allows rotation/positioning.
- Tension-suspended heated sphere — heat source held in space by tensile structure.

## Open questions

- What resistance materials are suitable for this form factor at domestic wattages?
- What is the minimum perforated ceramic wall thickness that maintains structural integrity at operating temperature?
- Is inductive power transfer at domestic heating wattages (750–2000W) viable without prohibitive coil size?
