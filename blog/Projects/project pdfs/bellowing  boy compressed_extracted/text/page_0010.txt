This new design maintained the inner
chamber being mostly spherical, how-
ever the top half was now a bubbly
afro instead of solid spiky hair and
wings. Whilst still being aesthetically
grounded it is able to double as a
removable lid. The legs were made
smaller than that of the previous
design but also staying relatively
in proportion to the new form. The
stance is that of a frog, with the
mouth facing the flies. The eyes
staying exotropic and upwards facing
to accentuate the playful absurdity
