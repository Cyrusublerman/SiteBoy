Negative Space
The creation of the framework
in turn creates negative spaces
between the joints. These spaces
are a main aesthetic component
of the resulting dome and can be
utilised to maximise function.
For these spaces to be used the
dimensions are to be discovered.
As each space is unique, this is
necessary to be calculated during
the point’s genesis.
Similar to the calculation of the
joints, the surrounding joint
locations are needed to be
assessed. For the identification
of the negative spaces’ corners, a
different variation of points are
needed.
The origin will form the topmost
point, two points will be derived
from the subsequent points on
both the origin joint’s curves. The
final “bottom” corner is discovered
by transposing one of the previous
two joints down one of their
denominators.
