Lattice Combination Examples
The algorithmically derived
points and dimensions allow
for the meeting of many design
requirements. If there is a need
to reduce material cost, the
structure can be generated to
have thin connecting poles of
minimal length.
These points all connect
in a manner that results in
structurally simple rhomboids.
This simplicity may also lead
to increased ease of use as
the points can be connected
in sequence based on spatial
position.
