Lattice Combination Examples
By simply connecting the points
in a domed lattice, the natural
geometry of phyllotaxis can be
instantly and easily observed.
The central connections,
composed of the first pole of
each curve - create a simplified,
pointed flower shape. The
surrounding points, spiralling
outwards, mimic the layering
of pine cones, ferns and roses.
Each hole between the points
creating a silhouette of a petal
or leaf.
The overlapping of similar but
unique curves in this structure
results in each silhouetted petal
also being unique, helping to
form the wanted organised
complexity of natural structure.
