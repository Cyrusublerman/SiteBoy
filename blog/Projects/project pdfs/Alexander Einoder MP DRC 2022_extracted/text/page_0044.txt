Lattice Combination Examples
By allowing for the adjustment of
point number, line denominators
and dome shape, the structure
can vary widely in complexity. As
long as the combination of curves
don’t intersect the same exact
location, the structure can become
complexly woven. Each point does
not need to necessarily connect
with the closest four and can
instead weave a further distance.
This increased complexity
retains the inherent aesthetics
of phyllotactic geometry as the
points retain their distribution.
Interesting experimentation
is possible with the connecting
curves as their structural
characteristics may vary
dramatically.
