Inner Material
The inner material will absorb most of the non-reflected sound. To achieve the
optimum absorbance, the material will require certain properties.
Firstly, the inner material needs to have high absorptive properties with varying
preferences in frequency spectra. Meeting these requirements in a sustainable
way would suggest the use of insulation materials created from either cellulose
or recycled cotton.
These two materials are formed in a sustainable way from renewable sources,
allowing for a relatively low impact in the manufacturing process. They are also
easily dyed to the bluish colour required for the water-like optical effect.
