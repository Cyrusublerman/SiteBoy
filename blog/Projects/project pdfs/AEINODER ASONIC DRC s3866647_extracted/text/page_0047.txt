The surface of the tile has potential to improve wellbeing through
several mechanisms. The functional aesthetics use the psychological
and neurological benefits of fractal imagery to reduce stress of
those within the environments. This is maximised by creating waved
structures with optimal fractal D values using parametric design.
The transparent, reflective nature of the outside material allows for
a dynamic modulation of the environmental light. These dynamics
reduce the stagnant sterility of the environment in a natural manner
that can aid in reducing fatigue and cognitive burden through light
subconscious stimulation.
As described previously, the tiles have the ability to reduce extraneous
noise, preventing the washing of reflected soundwaves by both
absorbing the energy of soundwaves and diffusing the sound that is
reflected.
The overall appearance and visual connection with the ocean has
the added benefit of structuring the emotional connection one has to
the environment. With the washing of oceans and seemingly moving
water over the walls, the novel environment and the tiles’ psychological
support will fuse into a more calming and positive memory of the
location. This, in conjunction with the other aspects of the design, will
elevate the ability of the space to promote wellbeing of all those within.
