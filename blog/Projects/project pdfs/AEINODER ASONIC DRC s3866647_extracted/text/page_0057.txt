The alternating wave density on the edges leads the wave generating
algorithm to create the appearance of directional movement. Like
water slowly lapping the shore the smooth parallel waves only slightly
disform and angle. Reducing the perpendicular wave number fewer
than three proved to remove the realism of the wave and moved the
shape further away from its needed fractal identity.
After forming the structure of the edges the inside waves can be
generated to create a smooth variety of peaks and troughs that flow
from one edge to the other, crossing over and fluctuating in height. As
long as the edge requirement is met then this inner wave structure can
have infinite possibilities.
