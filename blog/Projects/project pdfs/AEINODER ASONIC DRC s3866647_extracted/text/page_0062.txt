Outer Shell
The outer shell is required to be hard enough to hold the wave-tile’s structure,
without the porosity to absorb water and microbiota, and transparent enough to
allow for the light to pass through and simulate light’s interaction with water.
This outer-shell material must also have the least environmental impact as
possible whilst meeting the previously stated goals as well as maintaining a
relatively low cost.
These requirements would suggest the use of a transparent bioplastic. Made
from a range of renewable sources such as corn starch, many bioplastics can
be synthesised to have the necessary requirements and beneficially can return
to the manufacturing process or be broken down to environmentally safe
materials.
