Scattering Performance of Asonic Wave-Tile, Carlo Holden 2021.
Analysis of the sound mitigating effects of the Wave-Tiles showed
significant functionality (see above). The waved surface dispersed over
half the energy of the high frequency (>4kHz). It also showed a large
amount of scattering of the lower frequencies (<400Hz).
This is of high value to the hospital environment as extraneous noise in
these frequency ranges are indicative of synthetic sound, whether it be
from motors, air conditioning or electronic beeping.
