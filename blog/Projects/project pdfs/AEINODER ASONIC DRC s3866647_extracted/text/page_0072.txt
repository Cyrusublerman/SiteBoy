Analysis of Sound Diffusion
Using Grasshopper, team
member Carlo Holden
was able to simulate
the movement of sound
energy through a space
with and without the
Wave-Tile.
These simulations
highlight the increased
diffusion of reflected
sound particles and
overall reduced sound
energy levels
Increased numbers
of Wave-Tiles have
compounding effects on
the reduction of reflected
noise energy.
