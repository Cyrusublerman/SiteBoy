Ceiling Wave-Tiles, Lezhou Shen
Careful processing of the research and design goals
illuminated that three compositions were of most
importance. Firstly is the ceiling tile (see above). This
tile can replace the usual ceiling panels throughout the
hospital environment requiring relatively low cost of
installation and providing a high level of effect.
The second wave form is the corner piece (see below).
This corner wave is necessary to maximise the reduction
of low frequency noise in the environment.
Finally, the most iconic and highly important piece is
the standard wall tile (see across). This tile can work
seamlessly in conjunction with the other two forms and
can allow for an up-close and personal effect on those
within the hospital.
