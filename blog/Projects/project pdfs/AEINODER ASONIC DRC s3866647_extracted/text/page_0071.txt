The tiles are needed to be mounted in a fashion which removes the
need for gravity to hold the tile to the surface it is mounted on. The
tile must also be firm against the wall to allow for the transference of
sound-based energy into the wall. Also, to allow for ease of cleaning
and maintenance of the tile, the mounting mechanism must be
simple to detach and reattach.
Two designs were created to address these requirements. First was
the formation in which a loop is connected to a wall mounted hook
(see left). Additional padding pushes against the surface and creates
tension on the hook maximising stability.
The second design was that of push tabs that the outer plastic layer
snaps onto. This uses the natural elasticity of the structure to provide
omnidirectional support.
