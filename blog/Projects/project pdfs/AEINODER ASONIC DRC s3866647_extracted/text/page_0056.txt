Above is an image showing what was perhaps the most influential
prototype created. By converting a hand-drawn sketch into a
3-dimensional wave pattern, several epiphanies emerged. First is that
the core idea was possible. Second however, is that an even number
of waves on all edges creates a chaotic wave pattern that lacks the
wanted emotion. This lead to the development of a more linear wave
pattern that has significantly more peaks and valleys on two opposing
edges than the other two perpendicular to them.
The special sauce of the design is the creation of the tile edges (see
above). The ability to match up tiles seamlessly is imperative to the tiles
aesthetic function. Without the flowing of the waves from one section
to another, the tiles will fail to create the mesmerizing visual of an
ocean.
