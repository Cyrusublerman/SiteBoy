Environment and Mental Wellbeing
The environment that surrounds a person shapes their emotional and physical
state in many ways. From the emotional significance and stimulation of objects
and features to the subtleties of shape and form, the composition of our
surroundings change the way we think and feel (Gritzka et al. 2020).
Many different key attributes have been shown to have a direct effect on certain
aspects of mental wellbeing. Particularly that of stress and cognitive load
(Cooper, Boyko & Codinhoto 2009).
