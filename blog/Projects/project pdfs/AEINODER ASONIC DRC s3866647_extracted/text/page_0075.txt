The absorption of sound by the tiles also proved to be of significant
value, especially when paired with the diffusing properties of the tile.
Frequencies above 1000Hz easily passed though the plastic outer shell
and was absorbed into the cellulose foam.
Noticeable however is the lack of sound reduction between 400Hz
and 1000Hz. This is actually beneficial to the environment as it
allows for the first wave of human voice to travel, allowing for easy
communication between staff.
