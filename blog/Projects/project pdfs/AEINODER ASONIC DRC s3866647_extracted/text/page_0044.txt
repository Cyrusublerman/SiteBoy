Surface Structure
The surface of wavy water has
fundamental aspects that calm
and sooth the psychological state
of those who view it. The fractal
nature of the curves, valleys and
patterns of local minimums and
maximums create non-repeating
patterns that ride the line between
infinitely complex and intrinsically
understandable. This perfectly
natural form has been shown to
reduce stress, especially related to
noise-induced stress.
The waved surface breaks the
parallelity of the sound waves
as the variety of curved, waved
surfaces refract and reflect the
sound irregularly. This irregularity
causes the sound waves’ energy to
dissipate as they lose phase and
temporal alignment.
A spreading of phase causes the
sound waves to cancel each other
out and lose energy.
