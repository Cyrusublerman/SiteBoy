In one study, 84% of anesthetists reported that the
noise levels in their operating rooms negatively
affected their work (Katz 2014).
Patient safety and comfort can be adversely affected
by noise pollution, this can alter the required dosage
of anaesthetic agent.
A correlation has been identified between increased
sound levels during surgery and surgical-site
infections (Mcleod et al. 2021).
