Long term noise exposure has been linked to mental health
issues such as anxiety and depression (Zhou et al. 2020).
Studies have shown a significant correlation between ambient
noise levels and heightened stress (Liu et al. 2007).
Access to quiet spaces reduces annoyance to noise by
10-20% (Zoontjens & Cockings 2014).
Natural environments have been shown to reduce the
stress response to ambient noise (Thoma, Mewes &
Nater 2018).
