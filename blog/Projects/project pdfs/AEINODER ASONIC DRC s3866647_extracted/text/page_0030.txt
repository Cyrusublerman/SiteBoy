Operating Room Suite
Staff-related activities and conversations are a major
component of operating room noise and can produce
noise levels as high as 78 dBA .
Equipment-related noises as great as 120 dBA are
caused by movement of equipment, clanging, and
dropping of metal instruments, use of electric or air-
powered surgical instruments, hammers, suction
apparatus, anaesthetic monitors, and alarms (Mcleod
et al. 2021).
Forced air warming units alone add as much as 84 dBA
to the background noise.
Music can be up to 87dBA.
In one study conducted during cardiac surgery, there
was an average of 359 ± 158 alarms (1.2 min−1), 80% of
which had no therapeutic significance (Katz 2014).
