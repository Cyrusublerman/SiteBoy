D E S I G N D E C I S I O N S
The design of the physical structure was further refined with
experimentations in childish zoomorphic structures and the
stripping back of all unnecessary shape.
The form could be angular and sharp or soft and playful. Below is an
illustration of what was seen to be the minimal level of shape. This
has the angled body and aimed printer with only a few curves. The
only protrusions are the legs and the toggle switch. It now presents
itself like a small alien creature. Something that could even be
considered endearing or cute.
