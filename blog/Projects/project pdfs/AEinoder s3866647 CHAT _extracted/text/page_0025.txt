M I C R O P H O N E
For the audio input, a usb microphone (Audio Technica's AT9934) was gutted and
the internal componets were repurposed for the CHAT device.
The USB microphone requires minimal configuration to implement in the
Raspberry Pi and allows for minimal latency. It has an internal sound-card which
provides the gain control and analog to digital conversion so that the Raspberry Pi
merely needs to receive the digital signals and intergrate them into the speech to text
code.
