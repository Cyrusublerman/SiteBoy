Further refinement and progression in the technical aspects of this project lead
the design to become that presented above. Keeping the simple affordances and
signifiers and overall soft zoomorphic shape, the device is a balance of smooth
curves and simple geometry. A square front face allows the user to easily identify the
front and the back of the device. The camera is angled to run parallel with the surface
that the device is sitting on. The front legs remain soft so reaching between them will
have no level of intimidation or caution. The elctromagnetic field (EMF) antenna
protrudes proudly from the top, easily visible but not at all in the way.
A small size and curved back allows the device to be easily picked up and played with.
