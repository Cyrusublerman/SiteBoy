The body was printed in two components. This allows for a simple manufacturing process.
The flat sides and gentle curves minimised the supporting material needed to print, thus
reducing the cost.
The printer slides into the base of the stricture and the Pi is mounted on the lip above it. The front
has screw holes for the camera and the microphone PCB is slotted into the formed crevace and
glued.
The two pieces slot together, held tightly with screws through the aigned holes.
